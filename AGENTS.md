Read [`HANDOFF.md`](HANDOFF.md) first, then the latest correction at the end of
[`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md). [`ASSETS.md`](ASSETS.md) lists sources and hashes;
there is no required `~/shimano-analysis` dependency.

The active page is a bike-status reader. The current EU destination and the
absence of a validated US write are confirmed; synthetic tests do not prove
bike acceptance. Earlier A0 same-value staging was an unsupported inference.
Do offline bridge mapping or obtain a known-good write trace before proposing
another live mutation. Keep private passkeys, raw captures, serials, and vendor
binaries out of the public repository.
